How Wild Shape works in 2024 You keep your own Hit Points (131) — Beast form is no longer a second health bar. You gain 9 temporary HP on transforming, your AC becomes 15 if the beast's is lower, and you keep your Int/Wis/Cha, class features (Rage, Extra Attack) and feats. You use the beast's Str/Dex/Con, speeds and senses — so you lose your flight in all four of these forms. Max CR 1; no flying forms until Druid 8. Lasts 1 hour.
